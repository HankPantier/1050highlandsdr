Get outside

Trails, tees & chains.

Mountain bike · Run · Hike
Erie Singletrack

Roughly 5 miles of flowing, town-managed singletrack at Sunset Open Space — berms, rollers, and progressive green/blue/black skill lines with Front Range views. Ride it, run it, or link the loops a new way every time.

Town of Erie — Singletrack Trails → Trail map on Trailforks → Watch a ride-through →
Disc golf · Free to play
Coal Creek Disc Golf

An 18-hole, par-56 course rated 4.1★ on UDisc — elevation changes, mountain views, and a creek-fed water hazard to keep it honest. Free, dog-friendly, and about an hour and a half per round.

Course on UDisc → Town of Erie — course info → Course guide →
Championship golf
Colorado National Golf Club

Erie's premier public championship course (2700 Vista Pkwy) — home of the University of Colorado golf teams and host to NCAA and USGA events, with the Master's Restaurant for the 19th hole.
